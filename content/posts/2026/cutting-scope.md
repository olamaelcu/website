In lieu of wanting to focus more on [Livtet][] and [Beats][1], I had to sunset
both Spadat, the almost-complete Spades game over ATProto and Scratchback, the
SDK that enabled some of the primitives it was based on. I might revisit them
in the future when time and energy permits but for now they'll be retired.
Spadat was written using Godot and Rust and Scratchback entirely in Rust with a
SvelteKit frontend to allow game developers to tweak settings on-the-fly about
games as well as aiming to provide Web-based leader boards.

You also won't see them anymore in <https://waitlist.olamaelcu.net> if you
already subscribed to them. I apologize for the inconvenience.

To Livtet! To Beats!
