### The Plans

I'm aiming to release the things I've been hammering on for some time slowly to
everyone. The easiest way to be notified when a new service is available before
it's made more generally available is by [signing up for the waitlist][1]. Once
you're signed in and when spots are available, you'll be able to log into the
services you opted into. You can view all of the projects [for opting in][2]
&mdash; before you decide to commit.

### The Purpose

There's a bunch of things I want to build and contribute to and it's not enough
to _just write_ about it. There's enough space now seeing that I'm
([Jacky](https://www.jacky.wtf)) largely a [free
agent](https://www.jacky.wtf/work) and I'd like to spend that time working on
something I've written and engaged in for the last few years &mdash; the open
social Web. I'll have [time for folks who'd like to
talk](https://cal.com/jackyalcine) about ideas that'd like to bring to fruition around the social Web.

### The Process

The _ideal_ flow is a bit traditional: biweekly grind on features, concept
development and slinging that out to the public for commentary and review.
Until a more structured approach to voting on development is in place, I'll
reserve final say on what gets out there &mdash; mainly since this is all
effectively _pro-bono_ (for now).
